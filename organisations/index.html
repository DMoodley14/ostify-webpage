For organisations and charities

A virtual assistant for your service, built from your own content.

We build an assistant that answers people’s questions using only information you have approved. You send us your content, we build and test it with your clinicians, and it goes live on your website under your name after a short, supervised pilot.

  • Cyber Essentials certified
  • Documented to NHS standards
  • WCAG 2.2 AA accessible
  • Processed in the UK

At a glance

You make the decisions. We do the building and the running.

It only says what you approved

Answers from your content.

Your leaflets, pages and guidance, and nothing from the wider internet. When a question needs a person, the assistant says so and points to your team.

Urgent questions go to people

Your urgent-help message, first.

Anything that sounds urgent never reaches the AI. The person sees your own urgent-help wording straight away, pointing to 999, 111, a GP or your helpline. See the safety checks.

You stay in control

Your name, your content, your call.

You decide what it says and whether it is live, and you can take it down at any time. We can switch it off the same day.

How it works

Five meetings, from first conversation to launch.

Nothing reaches the public until your clinicians and your named lead have approved it.

  1. 1Discovery

    We learn what you want the assistant for and show you a working example. You then choose the content it will draw on.

  2. 2Scoping and build

    We agree the scope, build the assistant and send you a link to try. Two rounds of feedback and changes follow.

  3. 3Evaluation

    Every answer is tested, then reviewed by our clinical team and by two clinicians from your team. The safety and privacy documents are completed and signed.

  4. 4Pilot

    A small group uses it for 2 to 4 weeks. We review what they report with you and make any changes needed.

  5. 5Review and go live

    We review the pilot together and agree wider release. After launch, we re-test every quarter and send you a short report on anything that has changed.

Clinical safety

Do you need a Clinical Safety Officer?

Charities and independent services

No, you do not need to appoint one.

A Clinical Safety Officer is an NHS role. We still prepare your clinical safety record (DCB0129) and NHS digital assessment (DTAC) to the same standard, so the work is on record if a partner or funder asks.

NHS or local authority commissioned

Yes, and we plan for it together.

If any part of your service is commissioned by the NHS or a local authority, the requirements are stricter. Tell us at the start and we will plan for them with you.

What we need from you

Everything we will ask for, in one list.

  • Your content. The leaflets, web pages and guidance you want it to draw on.
  • Two clinicians. Working independently, to review a sample of test answers.
  • A named lead. One senior person who gives final approval.
  • Your urgent-help wording. What people see if their question sounds urgent.
  • Your branding. Logo, colours and a name for the assistant.
  • A pilot group. Staff, volunteers or members who use it first.

Questions from your trustees, data protection lead or IG team? Data flows, standards and certifications are on our trust and security page.
