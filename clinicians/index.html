01 / 07 · Build

Intended use. Ostify agents are built for NICE Evidence Standards Framework Tier B functions — informing and communicating with patients, not diagnosing conditions or directing treatment. What stays with you. On both plans you appoint your own clinical safety officer and complete your DCB and DTAC work.
