Your first agent

Under an hour, from leaflet to a working agent.

A typical first build. Evaluation and review take longer, and are worth the time.

  1. 0–10 minDescribe it

    Who it is for, what it helps with and where it stops.

  2. 10–25 minAdd your content

    Upload your leaflets and approve the passages it may use.

  3. 25–40 minSet how it behaves

    Reading level, tone, and the lines it must never cross.

  4. 40–60 minTry it on your phone

    Ask it what your patients ask, and refine until it sounds like your service.

Questions clinicians ask us

Before you build.

Can Ostify see my agent?

No. We cannot see your build or your evaluations until you submit them for review. Your agent, and every idea in it, belongs to you.

Who signs off what the agent says?

Your agent is yours, so clinical sign-off stays with you and your clinical safety officer. Ostify supports it with guardrails, evaluation tools and our published DCB0129. See assurance.

Can I use my trust’s leaflets?

Only add content that is yours or that you have permission to use. The builder asks you to confirm this before your agent can move on to evaluation.

Will patients know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The agent always says it is an automated assistant, not a person, even if you give it a friendly name.

Are patient conversations stored?

No. Conversations are processed in the UK and never stored. We log only metadata, such as which safety check responded.

Do I need approval from my organisation?

Usually, yes. Your agent is built in a private sandbox, so you can build and test first, then involve your clinical safety officer and information governance team before it reaches patients.

What does it cost?

Building is free, and always will be, for up to 2 MB of clinical content. Evaluation and assurance drafting come with the Founder plan, in beta. Live review is a one-off fee that varies, but is typically £1,000–£3,000, or nothing upfront through Osticellerate. See plans.
