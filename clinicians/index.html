01 / The workflow

Three steps, and
you are in every one.

BuildOsteoblast

Upload the documents your service already uses. Approve the passages your agent may draw on, one at a time.

EvaluateOsteoclast

Generate the questions your agent should face, prepare the answers you expect, and score what it actually says.

DocumentOsteocite

On Founder, Osteocite Assist drafts your assurance documents. You review them, sign them off and keep the decision.

What stays with you. On Hobbyist and Founder you appoint your own clinical safety officer and complete your DCB and DTAC work. Founder Plus includes an Ostify CSO, DCB0129 and DTAC.
