01 · What you can build

Three kinds of agent, each answering only from content you approve.

  1. 01

    Condition support and patient education

    A companion for the questions patients have between appointments, from your leaflets, guidelines and teaching.

    Is it normal to feel tired on this medication?
  2. 02

    Before and after a procedure

    Preparation and recovery questions that would otherwise come back to your team by phone.

    Can I shower after my knee replacement?
  3. 03

    Research participant information

    Help potential and enrolled participants understand your study, from your approved participant information sheet.

    How many visits will the study involve?

03 · What stays with you

You are the manufacturer. We are your development team.

Our tools support your safety case. Deciding your agent is safe for patients stays with you and your clinical safety officer.

You

  • Own the agent and all of its intellectual property
  • Provide and approve its content
  • Review its evaluation and sign off its documents
  • Hold clinical safety, with your own clinical safety officer
  • Complete your DCB and DTAC work

Ostify

  • Build and run the platform on Microsoft Azure
  • Provide the guardrails, Osti-gen and Osti-judge
  • Draft nine assurance documents through Osteocite
  • Publish our DCB0129 for your safety case
  • Review every agent before it goes live
  • Never see your build unless you invite us or submit it for review

04 · Questions

Common questions.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. You bring the clinical content and judgement. Ostify handles the technology, the safety checks and hosting.

What does it cost?

Building is free, and always will be. Evaluation and assurance drafting come with the Founder plan, currently in beta. Live review is priced app by app. See plans.

Can Ostify see what I build?

Not unless you give us permission or submit it for live review. Everything you build remains your intellectual property.

What kind of agent can I not build?

Ostify agents inform and communicate with patients. They do not diagnose, interpret symptoms or results, or direct treatment, and every agent declines those requests.
