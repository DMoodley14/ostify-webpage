What can you tell us?

Anything you noticed. For example:

  • A step where you were not sure what to do next.
  • An answer from your agent that was wrong, unclear or missing.
  • Wording or a label you did not understand.
  • Something that was slow, or did not load.
  • Something you expected to be able to do, but could not.
  • What you liked and would not want changed.

One note per thing you noticed is easiest for us to act on. Send as many as you like.
