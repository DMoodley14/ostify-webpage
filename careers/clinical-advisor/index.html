The opportunity

Clinicians know what their patients need. Most never get to build it. Digital tools are made for them, not by them, and good ideas stall waiting for budget, engineers or a supplier.

Ostify changes that. Clinicians use it to build safe, tested AI agents from their own approved content, with no coding and with safety and assurance built in. Along the way, they see exactly how their agent works: what it draws on, how it's tested and where its limits are. That understanding builds confidence, cuts the cost of bringing new ideas to life, and opens the door to innovation from the people closest to patients.

Too often, clinicians are brought in after a product is built, as a clinical product manager or a safety officer signing off someone else's design. Ostify is built the other way round, with clinicians at the centre from the start. Our clinical advisors help shape what we build and how it works, not just check it at the end.

What you'd do

  • Shape features and how agents behave, from early design through to release
  • Advise on clinical safety, patient needs and what good looks like in your specialty
  • Help us find colleagues and services interested in building or piloting
  • Lend clinical credibility as we talk to NHS organisations and investors

You might be

  • A practising clinician (doctor, nurse, AHP or pharmacist) in the NHS
  • Interested in digital health, AI or service innovation
  • Well connected within your specialty or trust

What you'd gain

  • A named advisor role, listed on our website
  • Early access to Ostify, and a real say in how it develops
  • Hands-on experience of how safe clinical AI is built, tested and assured
  • The chance to be part of something early, with clinicians at its centre

How it works

  1. A conversation about Ostify and how you could help.
  2. If it's a fit, we agree a light advisor arrangement that suits your time.
