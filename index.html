03 / How it works

Build, evaluate, assure,
then go live.

BuildOsteoblast

Define your agent and upload your clinical content: patient leaflets, guidelines, lectures, health promotion material and your own knowledge. Review how it is ingested, add guardrails, control how it looks, then talk to it on a simulated device or your own phone. This is the most important stage, so give it as much material and time as you can.

EvaluateOsteoclast

Run a fixed question set against your agent and score every answer for safety and quality. Osti-judge gives a first opinion; you and your colleagues have the final say.

AssureOsteocite

Osteocite Assist drafts nine assurance documents for your review and sign-off, and the dashboard holds every document your assurance process needs in one place.

Go liveLive review

Submit your agent and we review it individually before it can reach patients. Once approved, you get a link to share and tokens to run your pilot.

04 / Evaluation

An AI gives a first opinion.
Clinicians decide.

Generate your questions.

Osti-gen, our question model, drafts a set of patient questions for your agent. You review and edit them, then fix the set so every run is comparable.

Test every answer.

The questions are sent to your agent one by one and every answer is captured. Each agent also faces our standard safety set: emergencies, mental health crises, out-of-scope requests and attempts to trick it.

Judge, then overrule.

Osti-judge scores each answer for unsafe advice, missed emergency signposting, major clinical errors, missing essential information and incorrect sources, and rates accuracy, clarity, relevance, completeness, sourcing and usefulness. Its scores are advisory: you review every judgement and change any you disagree with.

Bring in colleagues.

Review by several clinicians is the gold standard. Send a review link to colleagues to reduce bias, then generate a report to share progress with stakeholders.

Guardrails

Every agent ships
with guardrails.

These layers run on every question, in order. You can add your own rules on top.

  • Emergency words. Words you choose trigger your emergency signposting immediately, before the AI sees the question.
  • Urgency detection. Catches emergencies described in everyday words, such as a drooping face or heavy bleeding, and signs of a mental health crisis.
  • No clinical decisions. Declines and refers requests to change a dose, diagnose, interpret symptoms or results, or give a prognosis.
  • No staff-only answers. Declines people who claim to be a doctor or pharmacist to get clinical information.
  • Harmful content screening. Questions and answers are screened for hate, self-harm, sexual and violent content. Self-harm always routes to crisis support.
  • Relevance gate. If your approved content doesn’t cover a question, the agent gives your out-of-scope message instead of guessing.
  • Your content only. Answers come only from your approved passages, never the model’s general knowledge, and cite only the sources you provide.
  • Fails safe. If a safety check is unavailable, the question is declined rather than answered.
  • Tested against manipulation. Every agent faces attempts to override its instructions, reveal its prompt or switch into an unrestricted mode.
  • Changes need re-approval. Changing your content or key settings takes a live agent back to review before patients see the change.

Reviewed by us
before patients see it.

An individual live review.

When you are happy with your build and evaluation, submit your agent for review. We check your content and guidelines, confirm the agent stays outside medical device territory, and test it for unsafe behaviour such as prompt injection.

A one-off review fee.

Live review is charged separately, typically £1,000 to £3,000 or more depending on your agent. Once approved, you get a link where patients can use it.

Tokens to pilot.

Your membership includes enough tokens to pilot your agent, and you can buy more as you need them. See plans and pricing.

Your clinical safety.

09 / Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can I deploy an agent as soon as I have built it?

No. After building and evaluating it, you submit it for an Ostify live review. Only approved agents can be reached by patients, and any later change to content or key settings needs approval again.

Who is responsible for clinical safety?

You are. You are the owner and manufacturer of your agent, and your clinical safety officer signs it off. We provide the tools, the guardrails and our published DCB0129 to support your safety case. See the responsibility breakdown.

Can Ostify help with assurance?

Osteocite drafts nine assurance documents for your review and gives you a dashboard to upload everything else your assurance process needs. While we build out our assurance service, we can offer additional support to some products. Apply at info@ostify.co.uk; we review every application.

What does going live cost?

Live review is a one-off fee, typically £1,000 to £3,000 or more depending on your agent. Your membership includes tokens to pilot it, and more can be bought as needed. See plans.

Can Ostify see my agent?

No, not unless you give us permission or submit it for live review. Everything you build remains your intellectual property.
