Ostify Clinical AI infrastructure for regulated health services

A safer way to answer patients’ questions.

Ostify lets clinicians and services turn the material they already hold, from patient information leaflets to protocols, pathways and service guidance, into conversational agents that answer only from that material and nothing beyond it.

Osteoblast

The builder. Ingests your own content and stands up a grounded agent, configured by the clinical team rather than a development team.

Osteoclast

The evaluator. Stress-tests every agent across in-scope, out-of-scope, crisis-adjacent and adversarial questions, and holds release until it passes.

Osteocite

The regulatory layer. Generates and maintains the governance documentation your organisation needs to deploy, and to keep deploying.

Cyber assurance
Cyber Essentials certification and independent penetration testing reports, shared on request.
