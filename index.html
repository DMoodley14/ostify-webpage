03 / Governance

The questions your
IG team will ask.

Built for transparency.

You select the source documents, approve the passages your agent may quote and evaluate its responses. Content review and clinical judgement remain central to the workflow.

Clear data flows.

Documented data flows explain how content is stored, accessed and retained, supporting your information governance review.

Safety controls.

Response checks and data minimisation are part of the product design, built for NICE Evidence Standards Framework Tier B functions — informing and communicating with patients, not diagnosing conditions or directing treatment. These controls support the clinical review and assurance required for deployment.

Security and compliance.

Work towards the Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT), Cyber Essentials and independent penetration testing is in progress. These activities are not yet complete. Contact us for current status and available evidence.

Building an agent is separate from deciding it’s ready for patient use. Review its intended purpose, evidence and the assurance responsibilities for your plan before deployment.
