safety measures across the service.

Assistants do not diagnose or give personal treatment advice. Urgent messages trigger your emergency message.

How Ostify keeps patients safe.

You stay in control

Your content, your name, your decision.

The assistant appears under your name and says only what you have approved. You decide whether it is live, and you can take it down at any time.

  • Your content, kept separateNo other organisation’s material can appear in your assistant.
  • Ostify does not store conversationsNo sign-in, and we do not keep the words of a conversation.
  • Drift analysis every 6 monthsWe re-test after launch and report what has changed.

Questions

Common questions.

What does it cost?

Organisations pay a single monthly plan that covers building, testing, the pilot and running the assistant. We agree the cost after a discovery call. Some charities may have the monthly fee waived for a year. Clinicians can use the builder free; see plans.

Why not use an off-the-shelf product?

Off-the-shelf tools are built for every service, so they fit none exactly. With Ostify, the assistant follows your pathway, your content and your rules, and you can change it whenever your service changes.

How long does it take to go live?

After the build and two rounds of changes, every answer is tested and reviewed by your clinicians. A small group then uses it for a 2 to 4 week pilot before wider release. See each step.

Do we need a Clinical Safety Officer?

Only if your service is commissioned by the NHS or a local authority. Otherwise you may not need one. We can help with your DCB0129 and DTAC documents on request, but Ostify does not act as your Clinical Safety Officer. More on clinical safety.

Who owns the assistant?

You own your content and how the assistant presents your organisation. We own and run the system underneath, and you decide what it says and whether it is live.

Where is data processed?

Patient conversations are processed in the UK, and Ostify does not store their words. Evaluation and assurance are processed within Europe. See our privacy policy.
