How it works

A simple process that asks little of your team.

You provide and approve the content. We set it up, test it and run it, and nothing reaches a patient until your clinicians have signed it off.

  1. 1Live demo

    We build an assistant from your content during the call, then send you a link to test in your own time.

    Your time: 20 minutes

  2. 2Build

    You share your content and references, and choose the tone, guardrails and appearance. We set up the rest.

    Your time: upload and review

  3. 3Evaluate

    We run 400+ questions and 10 simulated conversations. Your colleagues review answers through a shared link, and we repeat until every issue is resolved.

    Your time: clinical review

  4. 4Pilot

    A small group uses it once it behaves as intended, and we act on what they report.

    Your time: choosing the group

  5. 5Roll out

    Full launch under your name. We re-test every 6 months and report what has changed.

    Your time: none day to day

Every message

What happens when someone sends your assistant a message.

Each message passes a series of gates before anyone sees a reply. A message that is stopped gets wording your team approved, not an AI-written answer. Choose an example to watch it run.

The person types

I’m Sarah Jones, NHS number 485 777 3456. What should I bring to my appointment?

  1. Arrives securely
  2. Encrypted in transitProtected from the person’s phone to Ostify.
  3. Stays in the UKReceived by UK-hosted services, through one secure entrance.
  4. Checked before any AI writes a word
  5. Emergency phrasesKnown urgent phrases are caught first.
  6. Urgency in everyday wordsPhysical or emotional distress, even when it is played down.
  7. Clinical decisionsMedicines, diagnosis, results and prognosis go to your team.
  8. Harmful contentScreened by an independent service.
  9. Attempts to bypass the rulesTricks to make it ignore its instructions are refused.
  10. Answered from your content
  11. Personal details removedNames, contact details and NHS numbers are taken out.
  12. Matched to your approved contentIf nothing fits, it says so rather than guessing.
  13. Answer writtenOnly from your approved passages.
  14. Final checkChecked again before it is shown.

Questions

Common questions.

What does it cost?

Plans start at £100 a month, with conversations included. Charities and small organisations that want us to set up, evaluate and run the assistant with them start at £500 a month. Eligible independent UK health charities may have the monthly fee waived for their first year. See plans.

Do we need a Clinical Safety Officer?

Only if your service is commissioned by the NHS or a local authority. Otherwise you may not need one. We can help with your DCB0129 and DTAC documents on request, but Ostify does not act as your Clinical Safety Officer. More on clinical safety.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Report it to us at any hour, and you can remove the assistant from your website at any time. If a safety check is unavailable, the assistant declines to answer rather than carrying on without it. How we handle incidents and what can go wrong.

Who owns the assistant?

You own your content and how the assistant presents your organisation. We own and run the system underneath, and you decide what it says and whether it is live.

Where is data processed?

Patient conversations are processed in the UK, and Ostify does not store their words. Evaluation and assurance are processed within Europe. See our privacy policy.

Can a clinician build one for their own service?

Yes. Clinicians can build and preview an assistant for their own service free in the builder, then choose a plan when they are ready for patients. Ostify for clinicians.
