1 Build · Osteoblast · Free

Build it in
a private sandbox.

  • Define your agent: who it is for, what it helps with and where it stops.
  • Upload your content: patient leaflets, clinical guidelines, lectures, health promotion material and your own knowledge.
  • Shape how it learns: review how your content is split into passages and approve each one.
  • Add guardrails: ours are built in; add your own for your use case and see which are in force.
  • Design how it looks: name, colours, welcome message and tone.
  • Talk to it: on a simulated phone, or on your own device.

Spend the most time here. This is the most important stage. The more documents and detail you give your agent, the better it answers.

2 Evaluate · Osteoclast · Founder plan, in beta

An AI gives a first opinion.
Clinicians decide.

  1. Osti-genGenerateOur model drafts a set of patient questions for your agent.
  2. YouEdit and fixReview and edit the questions, then lock the set so runs are comparable.
  3. Your agentAnswerEach question is sent in turn through the real patient pipeline. Every answer is captured.
  4. Osti-judgeJudgeScores every answer against the safety and quality measures below.
  5. You + colleaguesReviewAccept or change every judgement. Send a review link to other clinicians.
  6. YouReportGenerate a report to share progress with stakeholders.

Safety checks

  • Unsafe answers
  • Missed emergency signposting
  • Major clinical errors
  • Missing essential information
  • Incorrect source

Quality scores

  • Accuracy
  • Clarity
  • Relevance
  • Completeness
  • Sources
  • Usefulness

Osti-judge is informational. It gives a detailed first opinion, but the clinician is always the expert. Reviews by several clinicians are the gold standard and reduce individual bias.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does it cost to build?

Building is free, always. Evaluation and assurance drafting come with the Founder plan, now in beta. Live review is a one-off fee, typically £1,000 to £3,000 or more depending on your agent, and your membership includes tokens to pilot it. See plans.

Can I deploy an agent as soon as I have built it?

No. You evaluate it, then submit it for an Ostify live review. Only approved agents can be reached by patients, and later changes to content or key settings need approval again.

Who is responsible for clinical safety?

You are. You are the owner and manufacturer of your agent, and your clinical safety officer signs it off. Our guardrails, evaluation tools, drafted documents and published DCB0129 support your safety case.

Can Ostify see my agent?

No, not unless you give us permission or submit it for live review. Everything you build remains your intellectual property.

Where is data processed?

Patient conversations are processed in the UK and never stored. Evaluation and assurance are processed within Europe. See our privacy policy.
