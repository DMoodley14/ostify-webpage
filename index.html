03 / Governance

The questions your
IG team will ask.

Built for transparency.

You pick the documents. You approve each passage the agent is allowed to quote, one at a time. You score the answers it gives. Nothing reaches a patient that you haven’t read and said yes to.

Clear data flows.

We tell you where your content sits, what the agent can reach, and what gets kept. Written down, so you can hand it to information governance without coming back to ask us.

Enterprise-grade safety.

Every response passes through our firewall before it reaches a patient, and we store as little as we can get away with. Less data held is less data to lose.

Compliance, in progress.

We’re working through the DSPT, Cyber Essentials and independent penetration testing so your team doesn’t have to. None of the three is finished yet — ask us and we’ll tell you exactly where each one stands.
