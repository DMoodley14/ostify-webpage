02 / Osteocite Assist

The nine documents.

Osteocite Assist is planned to draft these nine documents as part of Founder. Each draft requires your review and sign-off before it can form part of your assurance documentation.

  1. Intended Purpose Statement
  2. Device Classification Record
  3. Scope Boundary & Crisis Response Specification
  4. Source Document Register
  5. Clinician Review Record
  6. Evaluation Report
  7. Transferred Controls Acceptance
  8. Content Rights Warranty
  9. Change & Re-Review Record

03 / Complete assurance

Clinical safety support,
with an agreed scope.

Ostify agents are built for NICE Evidence Standards Framework Tier B functions — informing, communicating and supporting patient self-management — not Tier C functions such as diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Founder Plus and Enterprise are planned to provide an Ostify clinical safety officer and support for DCB0129 and DTAC. The scope of this service and the responsibilities retained by your organisation are agreed before delivery.

The service scope, deployment responsibilities and handover are agreed in writing before delivery. A service description does not constitute certification or a completed assessment of an agent.
