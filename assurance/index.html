Founder: nine documents, drafted with you.

Planned for Founder: Osteocite Assist uses our model to help draft these documents. Every draft needs your review and sign-off.

  1. Intended Purpose Statement
  2. Device Classification Record
  3. Scope Boundary & Crisis Response Specification
  4. Source Document Register
  5. Clinician Review Record
  6. Evaluation Report
  7. Transferred Controls Acceptance
  8. Content Rights Warranty
  9. Change & Re-Review Record
Your responsibility. You provide your own CSO and remain responsible for sign-off and completing the relevant DCB work and DTAC assessment. Drafting assistance does not constitute completed assurance or approval for deployment.

Both plans will offer a complete assurance solution including an Ostify clinical safety officer, DCB0129 and DTAC. Neither plan is available yet.

The detailed scope, deployment responsibilities and handover will be agreed before delivery. No certification or completed assessment is implied by these planned services.
