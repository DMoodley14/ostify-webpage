Built for the standards the NHS asks for.

The compliance evidence isn’t a step at the end. It’s produced by the same pipeline that builds and tests the agent.

What we supply.

  • DCB0129Hazard log and clinical safety case report for the Ostify platform, written to feed directly into your DCB0160 deployment work.
  • DTACA populated Digital Technology Assessment Criteria response, ready for your assessment team.
  • Data protectionICO registered. DPIA support, records of processing under UK GDPR, DSPT alignment, and UK data residency.
  • Cyber assuranceCyber Essentials certification and independent penetration testing reports, shared on request.
  • GroundingEvery agent answers only from the documents it was built from, cites its source, and declines anything beyond them.
  • EscalationRed-flag and crisis-adjacent messages are routed to a fixed escalation response, tested on every release.
Clinical safety officer. We hold DCB0129 responsibility for the platform. Your organisation appoints its own clinical safety officer for deployment under DCB0160.
