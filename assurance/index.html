Assurance

You own the safety case. We give you what you need to build it.

Guardrails in every agent, evaluation you can evidence, nine drafted assurance documents, our published DCB0129, and an individual review before anything goes live.

Build your ownCompare plans

01 · If you build it yourself

Your agent, your ideas. Our platform behind you.

When you build on Ostify yourself, everything you build belongs to you. If we are building an assistant for your organisation, see who does what instead.

Yours

  • Your agent, and every idea in it. The intellectual property is always yours
  • The content it draws on, and which passages it may use
  • Clinical sign-off, with a clinical safety officer if your service is commissioned by the NHS or a local authority
  • Your DCB and DTAC documentation, much of which we draft for you

How Ostify supports you

  • A secure health cloud environment to build, test and run your agent
  • Built-in guardrails, Osti-gen and Osti-judge
  • Nine assurance documents drafted for you to review
  • Our published DCB0129 to reference in your safety case
  • An individual review before your agent goes live

Nine documents drafted from your build and evaluation.

Each record is drafted from your agent. Read it, edit anything that isn’t right, then sign it as yours. The dashboard holds them alongside your own compliance documents, such as your DTAC form and DPIA.

  1. Intended Purpose Statement
  2. Device Classification Record
  3. Scope Boundary and Crisis Response Specification
  4. Source Document Register
  5. Clinician Review Record
  6. Evaluation Report
  7. Transferred Controls Acceptance
  8. Content Rights Warranty
  9. Change and Re-Review Record

After assurance comes review, a pilot and production. Each step is on how it works.
