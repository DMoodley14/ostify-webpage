02 / Osteocite Assist

Nine documents,
drafted with you.

On Founder, Osteocite Assist uses our model to draft each of these. Every draft needs your review and your sign-off before it counts for anything.

  1. Intended Purpose Statement
  2. Device Classification Record
  3. Scope Boundary & Crisis Response Specification
  4. Source Document Register
  5. Clinician Review Record
  6. Evaluation Report
  7. Transferred Controls Acceptance
  8. Content Rights Warranty
  9. Change & Re-Review Record
Drafting is not assurance. On Founder you provide your own CSO and remain responsible for sign-off, your DCB work and your DTAC assessment. A drafted document is a starting point for your review, not an approval to deploy.

03 / Complete assurance

When you would
rather we carried it.

Founder Plus and Enterprise include a complete assurance solution: an Ostify clinical safety officer, DCB0129 and DTAC. Where Founder hands you drafts to sign, these plans put the clinical safety responsibility with us.

The detailed scope, deployment responsibilities and handover are agreed in writing before delivery. Nothing here implies a certification or completed assessment for an agent you have not yet built.
