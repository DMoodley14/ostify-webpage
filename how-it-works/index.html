2 Evaluate Osteoclast · Founder plan, beta

Osti-judge gives a first opinion. Yours is the one that counts.

Every answer is scored by our judging model, then by you. Its judgement is there to help you read quickly, not to replace clinical review.

  1. GenerateOsti-gen drafts a set of realistic patient questions for your agent.
  2. Edit and lockYou review and edit the set, then fix it so every run is comparable.
  3. RunEach question goes through the real patient pipeline and every answer is captured.
  4. JudgeOsti-judge scores each answer on safety and quality.
  5. ReviewYou and at least one other clinician accept or overrule every score, using a shared review link.
  6. ReportShare an evaluation report with your stakeholders.

Choose how many questions to generate. Emergency, boundary and out-of-scope questions are always included, with a fixed safety core on top.

Safety checks

  • Unsafe answers
  • Missed emergency signposting
  • Major clinical errors
  • Missing essential information
  • Incorrect source

Quality, scored 1 to 5

  • Accuracy
  • Clarity
  • Relevance
  • Completeness
  • Sources
  • Usefulness

Every evaluation needs at least two clinicians. Review by more than one clinician is the gold standard, and the simplest way to reduce individual bias.

3 Assure and comply Osteocite · Founder plan, beta

Nine documents drafted from your build and evaluation.

Osteocite Assist writes the first drafts for you to review and sign off. The dashboard holds them alongside everything else your assurance process needs. Agents built on Ostify are aligned with DSPT and DTAC, and our own DCB0129 is published for you to reference.

Each record is drafted from your agent. Read it, edit anything that isn’t right, then sign it as yours.

  1. Intended Purpose Statement
  2. Device Classification Record
  3. Scope Boundary and Crisis Response Specification
  4. Source Document Register
  5. Clinician Review Record
  6. Evaluation Report
  7. Transferred Controls Acceptance
  8. Content Rights Warranty
  9. Change and Re-Review Record

4 Submit for review Live review · One-off fee

No agent reaches a patient until we have reviewed it.

We check your content and guidelines, confirm the agent stays outside medical device territory, and try to make it misbehave, including with prompt injection. Approval is not a safety guarantee. That decision stays with your clinical safety officer.

Submit when you are ready. The readiness checklist shows what the reviewer will look at.

Review fee
Typically £800–£1,000A one-off fee that varies with your agent’s size and scope. We will quote before we start, or apply for Osticellerate to pay nothing upfront.
