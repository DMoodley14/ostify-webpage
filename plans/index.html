02 / Before you choose

Building an agent and
deploying one are
different decisions.

Every plan lets you build and evaluate. None of them decides on your behalf that an agent is ready for patients — that judgement rests on its intended purpose, the evidence behind it, and the assurance work your deployment requires.

The plan you choose determines how much of that assurance work you do yourself. Hobbyist includes none of it. Founder drafts the documents for your sign-off. Founder Plus and Enterprise include an Ostify clinical safety officer, DCB0129 and DTAC.

Talk to us about what your deployment needs. An enquiry is not a purchase and creates no commitment.
