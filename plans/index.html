04 · Questions

Common questions.

Is the builder really free?

Yes, up to 2 MB of clinical content in total, with no time limit.

How is the review priced?

It is free if your agent is accepted for Osticellerate. If not, it is a one-off fee, typically £1,000–£3,000, depending on the size and scope of your agent. We will quote before we start. If you change your content or key settings, the agent is reviewed again before patients see the change.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing extra. Approval comes with a dedicated patient link and a generous token allowance for your pilot.

How does production pricing work?

Choose a package with a set number of patient conversations each month, or pay-as-you-go. Both are prepaid, so you pay before you use it.

What is Osticellerate?

A programme for agents with a strong use case. Apply, and if we accept, we move your costs to the live phase: nothing to pay before your first patient, then you pay as you scale.
