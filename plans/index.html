Questions

Common questions.

Is the builder really free?

Yes, up to 10 MB of clinical content in total, with no time limit. You can preview your assistant on your own phone, through the same safety checks a patient’s question goes through. Sharing it with patients needs a paid plan.

What counts as a conversation?

About ten message turns with the assistant. The allowance is really measured by usage: shorter questions and answers use less, so you get more conversations; longer ones use more, so you get fewer. All conversation numbers are approximate.

What happens if our allowance runs out?

Buy a bundle to keep the assistant running. If the allowance is used up and no bundle has been bought, the assistant pauses until you top up. Nothing is charged automatically.

What is a deployment review?

A full review of your assistant before it goes live, or after a change such as new content. The Clinician plan includes one a year and the Small organisation plan two; further reviews can be added.
