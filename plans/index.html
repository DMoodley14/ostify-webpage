Questions

Common questions.

Is the builder really free?

Yes, up to 2 MB of clinical content in total, with no time limit.

Can I try my agent before I pay anything?

Yes. On the free plan you can scan a code and use your own app on your phone, through the same safety checks a patient’s question goes through. The preview is for you and your colleagues; sharing it with patients comes after review.

What does Founder add?

Evaluation with Osteoclast, assurance documents drafted by Osteocite, and a larger content allowance. It is in beta, so contact us to join.

When do I start paying?

Only when you take your agent towards patients. The review, your link and running costs are on Pilot and beyond.
