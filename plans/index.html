Plan details.

Are there pilot results?

There is no current pilot. The walkthrough and illustrative example explain the product; they are not evidence of clinical outcomes.

What will the paid plans cost?

Paid pricing, commitments and onboarding times have not been announced. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

What changes with Founder Plus or Enterprise?

Both are planned to include a complete assurance solution with an Ostify CSO, DCB0129 and DTAC. Neither is available yet.

What will Founder include?

Osteocite Assist will help draft nine documents. You are responsible for reviewing and signing them off, providing your own CSO, and completing DCB and DTAC work.
