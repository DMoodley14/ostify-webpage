03 · Questions

Common questions.

Is the builder really free?

Yes, up to 2 MB of clinical content in total. Building, guardrails and testing on a simulated phone cost nothing, with no time limit.

How do I join the Founder beta?

Contact us at info@ostify.co.uk or through the contact form.

How is the live review priced?

It is a one-off fee that varies, but is typically £1,000–£3,000, depending on the size and scope of your agent. We will quote before we start. Through Osticellerate, strong use cases can pay nothing upfront.

What is Osticellerate?

A programme for agents with a strong use case. Apply, and if we accept, we move your costs to the live phase: nothing to pay before your first patient, then you pay as you scale.

Can Ostify see my agent?

No. We cannot see your build or your evaluations until you submit them for review. Everything you build is yours.

Who signs off clinical safety?

Your agent is yours, so sign-off stays with you and your own clinical safety officer. We support it with our guardrails, tools and published DCB0129. See assurance.
