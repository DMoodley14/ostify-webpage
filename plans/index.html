02 · Pilot and beyond

From submission to your own link, and on from there.

The pilot starts when you submit your agent for review. It ends with a link of your own to share with patients, staff or the public.

1Submit for reviewFree with OsticellerateOtherwise typically £800–£1,000We review your agent individually. Free if it is accepted for Osticellerate. If not, a one-off fee, quoted before we start, that varies with your agent’s size and scope.

03 · What a conversation costs

About 10 conversations for £1.

After your pilot allowance, you pay for the messages your agent answers. A message is one question and its answer.

Price
About 10 conversations of 12 messages for £1That is around 10p per patient for a full conversation, or a little under 1p a message. Shorter conversations cost less.
What you are paying for
The AI’s reading and writingFor every message, the agent rereads its instructions and the passages from your content that fit, then writes a reply. Longer passages and longer answers cost a little more, shorter ones a little less.
How you pay
In advance, never automaticallyYou top up before you use it, so there are no surprise bills.
Where the price is going
About 65 conversations for £1, within a monthThat would bring a full conversation down from around 10p per patient to under 2p. We are rapidly improving the efficiency of our models. Our aim is to use smaller, smarter models that give the same quality of answer for less, and to pass the saving on.

04 · Questions

Common questions.

Is the builder really free?

Yes, up to 2 MB of clinical content in total, with no time limit.

How is the review priced?

It is free if your agent is accepted for Osticellerate. If not, it is a one-off fee, typically £800–£1,000, depending on the size and scope of your agent. We will quote before we start. If you change your content or key settings, the agent is reviewed again before patients see the change.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing beyond the review. Approval comes with your own link and a generous allowance of messages.

What happens when my pilot allowance runs out?

You top up in advance, at about 10 conversations of 12 messages for £1. Nothing is charged automatically.

What is Osticellerate?

A programme for agents with a strong use case. Apply, and if we accept, we move your costs to the live phase: nothing to pay before your first patient, then you pay as you scale.
