Your enquiry

The enquiry form sends the name, email address, plan and message you enter directly to us through a processor, Microsoft Azure Communication Services (UK data location), which delivers it to our inbox. Those details allow us to respond to your enquiry. If that service is unavailable, the form instead prepares an email for you to send yourself through your own email application. Please do not send patient information.

Website services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to the hosting and font services to load the site. No analytics service is currently active. If we enable one in future, it will be Microsoft Azure Application Insights, it will run only after you accept an on-page prompt, and it will not run at all if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Product data

Ask us for the applicable product privacy information and data-processing arrangements before providing personal or clinical data through a product workflow.
