Who we are

Ostify Health Ltd (“Ostify”, “we”, “us”) is a company registered in England and Wales under company number 17433177, with its registered office at 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for handling it in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR).

For any question about this policy or your personal information, email info@ostify.co.uk.

What this policy covers

This policy covers personal information we collect through this website, ostify.co.uk, and through enquiries you send us.

It does not cover the Ostify product or any clinical or patient information processed within it. Use of the product, and any processing of personal or health data it involves, is governed by the agreement and data processing terms we put in place with each customer. Please contact us for that information before providing any personal or clinical data through a product workflow.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use the enquiry form or email us, we collect:

  • your name and email address;
  • the plan you are interested in; and
  • the content of your message, and any further correspondence with us.

Please do not include patient information, health information or other confidential clinical details in an enquiry. The website is not designed to receive it.

Information collected when you visit the website

  • Technical information. Like any website, the servers that deliver this site receive your IP address, browser type, the page you requested and the date and time of the request. This is needed to deliver the site and protect it from misuse.
  • Analytics information, only with your consent. If you accept analytics cookies, we collect information about how you use the site: the pages you view, the page you came from, how long you stay, your browser and device type, and your approximate location (country and city) derived from your IP address. Our analytics service is configured not to keep your full IP address.
  • Your cookie choice. We store whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies in your browser, so we don’t ask you again on every page.

We do not use the website to collect special category data, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

We will not send you marketing emails unless you have asked us to.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work in a comparable way. We use them sparingly, and we only set analytics cookies if you accept them.

Managing your choice

You can accept or reject analytics cookies when you first visit the site, and change your mind at any time using Cookie settings, which is also linked at the bottom of every page. If you reject analytics cookies after accepting them, we delete them.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we treat it as a rejection and do not set analytics cookies. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the website and our business, who act on our instructions and are bound by contract to protect it:

  • Microsoft, which provides the service that delivers enquiries from our form to our inbox (Azure Functions and Azure Communication Services) and our analytics service (Azure Application Insights). These services are configured to store data in the UK.
  • GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft company), which hosts this website and receives the technical information needed to deliver it.
  • Our email provider, which stores the enquiries and correspondence we receive.

We may also share information with professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants; with a buyer or investor as part of a sale, merger or restructuring of our business, under appropriate confidentiality terms; or where we are required to by law, or need to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of others.

International transfers

We aim to keep personal information in the UK. Some of our service providers, including the infrastructure used to host and deliver this website, may process information outside the UK, including in the United States.

Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it is protected by a safeguard recognised under UK data protection law. These include adequacy regulations, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified organisations, or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum issued by the Information Commissioner’s Office. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Enquiries and correspondence: up to 24 months after our last contact with you. If you become a customer, we keep relevant information for as long as our agreement requires and for any period required by law afterwards.
  • Analytics information: up to 90 days, after which it is deleted automatically.
  • Technical information: for the limited period our hosting provider needs to deliver and secure the website.

We may keep information for longer where we are required to by law, or where it is needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls limited to the people who need access, and service providers selected for their security practices. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any incident.

Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information;
  • object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask us to transfer information you provided to us to another organisation, where technically feasible; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

Some of these rights apply only in certain circumstances. To exercise any of them, email info@ostify.co.uk. We may need to confirm your identity before we respond. We will reply within one month, and there is usually no charge.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we change the services we use. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Where a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will make it clear on this website.

This website is intended for healthcare professionals and organisations, and is not directed at children.
