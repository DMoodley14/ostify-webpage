About Ostify.

Ostify helps clinicians turn approved source material into patient-facing agents, with tools for content review, response evaluation and deployment preparation.

01 / The founder

The founder.

Divyan Moodley, Founder.

Divyan is a medical doctor and software engineer. He trained in medicine in Cape Town, South Africa, and qualified as a general practitioner in 2023, before moving to the UK to practise as a specialty doctor in HIV medicine for three years.

Alongside clinical work he completed an MSc in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence at the University of York, and built MACH, a multilingual conversational agent for people living with HIV, now in a pilot at an NHS hospital.

His interests are digital health and the evaluation of health AI. He publishes in the field and reviews digital health papers for academic journals. Ostify grew out of that work: the tooling clinicians need to take approved material to a patient-facing agent, and to show how it behaves before it reaches anyone.

For questions about Ostify, contact info@ostify.co.uk.

02 / Product development

Our current stage.

The builder is free for everyone, always. Evaluation and assurance documentation drafting are in beta testing through Founder, and further clinical safety support is in development, with scope agreed directly with us.

Ostify is at an early stage of development. Pilot outcomes, customer case studies and certifications are not yet available. This website outlines the product workflow, planned services and deployment responsibilities.
