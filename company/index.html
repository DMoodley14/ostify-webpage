About us

Built by clinicians, for healthcare organisations.

We make approved health information easier to reach, with the clinical discipline healthcare demands.

The story

Why we built Ostify.

People are increasingly asking AI their health questions instead of searching, and the websites and leaflets health organisations work hard on can go unread. General AI services are not evaluated against an organisation’s approved content, and they are not tailored to its patients or its service.

Everyone agrees healthcare software must be built safely. Our argument is that safety should be the foundation, not a step at the end. Where much healthtech development builds the product first and assesses the risks later, we start with the hazard log and work backward.

That is where the name comes from. In the body, bone is laid down, reshaped and matured to form the skeleton. Ostify works the same way: Osteoblast builds, Osteoclast evaluates and modifies, and Osteocite brings the maturity.
