You have seen what needs fixing. Build the company that fixes it.

Our mission

To empower clinicians to build great tech.

Ostify was started by a clinician, for clinicians. Read the story.

Why you

You already have the hardest part.

Technology can be learned or borrowed. Knowing what patients ask, where a pathway breaks and what a safe answer sounds like takes years at the bedside. That is what a great product is built on.

Build

No developer. No funding round.

Build your first agent yourself, for free, from the content you already trust. The engineering, hosting and safety checks are already done.

Own

The idea and the product stay yours.

Everything you build belongs to you, and we cannot see it until you submit it for review. How we protect your ideas.

Grow

Take it wherever the need is.

Offer it to NHS trusts, bring it to your own clients, or run it as a not-for-profit initiative. What helps your patients can help thousands more.

Questions founders ask us

Before you build.

Who owns what I build?

You do. Your agent and every idea in it remain your intellectual property.

Do I need a developer or a technical co-founder?

Not to build, evaluate or pilot. The builder needs no code, and hosting, guardrails and security are part of the platform. See what is under the hood.

Who is this for?

Clinicians. Ostify is built for people who know a service from the inside and want to build something of their own. You will need at least one clinical colleague to review your evaluation with you, and a clinical safety officer to sign it off.

What do I pay before my first user?

Building is free. The review before going live is a one-off fee, or nothing upfront if your agent is accepted for Osticellerate. See pricing.
