The product

The platform behind every assistant.

The same tools, from first upload to a tested assistant. No code, and nothing reaches a patient until it has been reviewed.

  1. 1
    Your content in

    Upload leaflets, guidelines or policies, or write your own question-and-answer pairs.

  2. 2
    Only what you approve

    Every file is split into passages. The assistant can only use the ones you sign off.

  3. 3
    Readiness at a glance

    See how close your assistant is to ready, step by step.

  1. 1
    Choose the voice

    Reading level, warmth and answer length, with a live sample as you change them.

  2. 2
    See it before patients do

    A live preview shows how answers will read. Then scan a code to try your own app on your phone, free.

  3. 3
    Lines it never crosses

    No diagnosis, no personal treatment advice. Add your own rules on top.

  1. 1
    Questions written for you

    Emergencies, edge cases and attempts to trick it, plus a fixed safety core.

  2. 2
    Choose how thorough

    From a quick 30-question check to a 300-question run.

  3. 3
    Every run kept

    Compare runs over time and sign off when you are happy.

What you can build

What an assistant can do for your service.

Four examples. Every assistant answers only from content you have approved.

  • Patient-facing
  • Public-facing
  • Staff-facing
  1. 01
    Patient-facing

    Condition support and patient education

    A companion for the questions patients have between appointments, drawn from your leaflets, guidelines and teaching.

    Can I still exercise after my DEXA scan?

    Yes. A DEXA scan does not change what you can do afterwards.Your DEXA scan · approved

  2. 02
    Patient-facing

    Before and after a procedure

    Your service’s own preparation and recovery instructions, available at 2am, without another call to the ward.

    When do I need to stop eating before my colonoscopy?

    Your appointment letter gives the exact time. The preparation guide explains each step.Preparing for your colonoscopy · approved

  3. 03
    Public-facing

    Research participant information

    Let potential and enrolled participants ask about your study, answered from your approved participant information sheet.

    Can I leave the study if I change my mind?

    Yes. You can withdraw at any time, without giving a reason.Participant information sheet · approved

  4. 04
    Staff-facing

    Staff induction and local policies

    Upload your local policies and guidelines, and new and existing staff can find what they need through a simple chat, any time of day.

    Who do I escalate to if a patient deteriorates overnight?

    Bleep the on-call registrar through switchboard, then follow the deteriorating patient policy.Deteriorating patient policy · approved

Guardrails

What happens to every question a patient asks.

Part of our 33 safety measures, and you can add your own rules on top.

  • Emergency words

    Urgent phrases trigger your emergency message.

  • Urgency detection

    Catches emergencies in everyday language.

  • No clinical decisions

    Declines dose, diagnosis and prognosis questions.

  • Harmful content

    Screens messages and answers.

  • Relevance

    No guessing when your content does not cover it.

  • Your content only

    Answers from your approved passages.

See every guardrail in detail or read how safety works overall.
