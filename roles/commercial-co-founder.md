Ostify is built. The next step is getting it to the clinicians and services who need it. I am looking for someone to lead that with me.

## What you would do

- Bring Ostify to clinicians, services and NHS organisations, and turn interest into pilots
- Shape pricing, partnerships and how we sell into the NHS
- Lead fundraising and grant applications alongside me
- Build the commercial side of the company from the start

## Who you might be

- Experience selling to, or working in, the NHS or health and care
- Comfortable with long sales cycles, procurement and partnerships
- Interested in safe, clinician-led innovation, not hype
- Ready to build something from an early stage

## The honest terms

Ostify is not yet funded, so this role is not salaried at first. In return, you would hold a meaningful co-founder stake, agreed together with standard vesting so it is fair to both of us. Salary comes in as funding or revenue allows.

We can begin part-time and agree how it grows. We work remotely across the UK, with time together in London when it helps.

## How it works

1. **A conversation** about the vision, what you would bring, and what you want from it.
2. **A small project together** for a few weeks, so we both know how we work together.
3. **Then the agreement:** equity, vesting and roles, written down properly.
