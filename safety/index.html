Safety

An assistant your clinicians can stand behind.

It answers from the content you approve. It is designed to point people to help when they may need it. And nothing reaches patients until your team has tested and signed it off.

Talk to usSee how it works

Urgent help

If someone may need urgent help.

Several independent checks look for urgent messages before any answer is written.

  1. Everyday words count

    The checks are designed to catch messages like “my lips have gone a funny colour”, not just clinical terms.

  2. Your approved emergency message

    When a check fires, the assistant shows your wording.

  3. No judging, no reassuring

    It does not decide how serious things are, and it does not try to reassure.

Approved content

It sticks to what you have approved.

Your assistant is instructed to answer only from your content.

  1. No relevant content, no attempt

    If nothing relevant is found, it will not attempt an answer. If it does not know, it says so.

  2. No clinical decisions

    It declines doses, diagnosis, test results and prognosis. Saying “I’m a nurse” does not change that.

Control

Your clinicians stay in control.

Patients only see a version that has been through your checks.

  1. Clinical sign-off

    It cannot be submitted without clinical sign-off and a finished evaluation.

  2. A locked version

    The approved version is sealed and reviewed before it is published.

  3. A separate patient system

    Unfinished drafts cannot reach patients.

  4. Reversible releases

    A published version can be rolled back in one click.

Evidence

See the evidence for yourself.

Test answers are assessed for safety, accuracy, consistency, unnecessary refusals and tone.

  1. Clinicians decide

    An AI reviewer can flag problems, but your clinicians decide.

  2. Honest results

    Results show a likely range as well as a headline score, because small tests should not look more certain than they are.

  3. Retest after a fix

    Ask the same questions again and see what changed.

Accountability

Built for your safety file.

Your organisation stays responsible for its assistant, including under DCB0129.

  1. Forms and drafting support

    Ostify provides the platform, along with support for your DTAC, DPIA, safety plan, safety case and hazard log.

  2. Built by a doctor

    Ostify was built by a GMC-registered doctor and trained Clinical Safety Officer, and is certified to Cyber Essentials.

If something fails

What happens if something fails?

If a safety service stops working, the assistant declines rather than answering without checks. This means it may not always show the emergency message during an outage, so your service should always keep its usual routes to urgent help.

Ostify provides general information from approved content. It does not diagnose or replace clinical care, and cannot guarantee that every urgent message will be detected. In an emergency, call 999.
